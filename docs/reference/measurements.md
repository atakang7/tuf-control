# Measurements

Seven days on an ASUS TUF Gaming A16 FA607NUQ. Values are averages recorded with stock firmware and the author's profiles.

| Metric | Stock firmware | TUF Control |
|---|---:|---:|
| Plugged in, CPU temperature | 59.1 C | 55.7 C |
| Plugged in, CPU fan | 2,907 rpm | 2,372 rpm |
| On battery, CPU fan | 5,185 rpm | 771 rpm |
| On battery, power draw | 14.4 W | 10.9 W |

Recorded duration: 5.6 hours plugged in and 0.5 hours on battery with stock firmware; 73.7 hours plugged in and 1.5 hours on battery with TUF Control.

With a 15 W plugged-in profile, mean CPU clock was 2.8 GHz compared with 3.3 GHz under stock settings. The recording periods were unequal.
